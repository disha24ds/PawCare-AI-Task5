# 🐾 PawCare AI — AI for Social Good

## 📌 Project Overview

PawCare AI is a proposed AI-powered animal rescue and welfare system designed to help people report stray, injured, sick, or abandoned animals and connect them with animal rescue organizations.

The project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) can support animal welfare and contribute to positive social change.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many stray and abandoned animals experience injuries, illnesses, hunger, and neglect. Animal rescue organizations may struggle with limited resources, delayed reports, and difficulties prioritizing urgent cases.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

PawCare AI proposes an intelligent platform with the following features:

* 📷 Animal rescue reporting through photographs and descriptions.
* 🤖 AI-assisted image analysis to flag possible visible injuries.
* 🚨 Suggested priority levels for rescue requests.
* 📍 Location-based rescue coordination.
* 📋 Rescue case tracking.
* ❤️ Animal welfare awareness.

Human review is essential for urgent cases. AI predictions are not veterinary diagnoses.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. A person finds an animal in need.
2. They submit a photograph, location, and description.
3. The AI analyzes the report for possible warning signs.
4. The system suggests a priority level.
5. A rescue coordinator reviews the report.
6. The relevant rescue organization or veterinary service is contacted.
7. The case status is updated after assistance.

## 🌍 Expected Social Impact

* Support the identification of urgent rescue cases.
* Improve coordination among citizens and animal welfare organizations.
* Help rescue teams organize limited resources.
* Encourage community participation.
* Promote responsible animal care.

## 🔐 Ethical Considerations

* Protect users' personal information and location data.
* Reduce AI bias by using diverse training data.
* Monitor false predictions and missed urgent cases.
* Keep humans involved in important rescue decisions.
* Avoid presenting AI predictions as veterinary diagnoses.

## 🚀 Future Scope

* Support for regional languages.
* Integration with verified animal rescue helplines.
* Adoption and fostering services.
* Animal vaccination and sterilization tracking.
* Animal welfare monitoring dashboards.
